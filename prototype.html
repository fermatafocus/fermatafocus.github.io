our prototype


what fermata does

fermata creates custom focus sessions with a curated playlist based on the task at hand. the goal is to help students choose audio that supports performance — especially for reading-heavy work.

core features

  • task selection (reading, problem-solving)
  • session timer for structured focus
  • curated playlists matched to task
  • progress tracking & session history

research-driven

based on our findings, fermata recommends instrumental or no music for reading comprehension while allowing flexibility for other task types.
