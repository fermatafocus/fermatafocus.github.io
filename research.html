takeaway

comprehension declined most when participants listened to lyrics they understood, suggesting semantic (language) processing competes with reading.

why lyrics interfere

reading relies on language processing. lyrics also require language processing. when both happen at the same time, cognitive load increases and comprehension can drop— especially when the lyrics are semantically understood.

time + effort
  • lyrics increased distraction and cognitive effort
  • english lyrics showed the strongest interference
  • instrumental aligned closest with silence

even when students feel “fine” studying with music, performance can still change—especially for reading-heavy tasks.

what this means for students

  • choose instrumental or silence for reading-heavy tasks
  • avoid native-language lyrics when comprehension matters
  • foreign lyrics may be less disruptive than understood lyrics
  • match your music to your task type
